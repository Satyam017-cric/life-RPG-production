THE DESIGN

Remove the delay between effort and reward.

Life RPG is designed around a simple game loop: define an action, finish it, receive immediate feedback, and watch your identity change over time.

AUTH

Private by default.

Passwords are hashed with Werkzeug and sessions are server-managed. Quest and character queries are scoped to the authenticated user.

PROGRESS

Server-calculated rewards.

XP, Gold, levels, streaks and attribute increases are calculated on the backend so the browser cannot simply rewrite the character sheet.

DATA

Built for real persistence.

The app supports PostgreSQL in production and SQLite locally. Render connects the web service to a persistent PostgreSQL database.
